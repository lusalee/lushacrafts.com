About Dongguan Lusha Crafts Co., Ltd.

We are a Dongguan-based artificial plants factory supplying B2B buyers worldwide. On this new-arrivals site you will find 145 factory-direct designs, with photos taken in our own production facility. Request a quote, samples or a factory visit using the contact details below.

What we offer

Factory-direct pricing with no middlemen. Every design on this site is photographed in our own facility — what you see is what we ship. We handle custom sizes, colours and packaging for wholesale and project orders.

Quality & compliance

Materials are checked at intake, during assembly and before shipment. SGS test reports for UV resistance and material safety are available on request. Standard lines carry a 3-year UV guarantee.
